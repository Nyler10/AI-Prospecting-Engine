# ICP Criteria — Streaming Media Sales (Worked Example)

A concrete instance of the [ICP framework](../../docs/icp-criteria.md), applied to selling an OTT/streaming platform.

## Target profile

- Organizations running **live events or sports coverage** that currently lack a strong connected-TV presence
- **Content-first organizations** (e.g., YouTube-first creators, media companies) sitting on a substantial content library with no dedicated streaming home
- Companies already operating their **own VOD platform** who could benefit from a more centralized or reliable delivery layer

## Hard qualifying tests

1. Does the organization have a demonstrable, ongoing content pipeline (live events, a video library, or regular original programming) — not just a one-off video presence?
2. Is there an observable distribution gap — e.g., web-only presence with no connected-TV apps (Roku, Fire TV, Apple TV) — that the product directly closes?

## Priority signals

- Recent public recognition (e.g., an industry award for programming quality) — signals both content volume and budget tier
- Geographic or reference-client proximity to an existing customer in the same sub-vertical

## Exclusions

- **Segment exclusion:** community/volunteer-run media organizations follow a fundamentally different buying process than city government departments or commercial media companies and should not be pitched with the same script
- **Ownership exclusion:** any account already owned by another rep in the shared CRM is excluded from new outreach regardless of fit

## Verification discipline

Domain and platform status are left as `unknown` in working batch files rather than entering unverified data into the CRM.
