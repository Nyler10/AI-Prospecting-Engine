# Pitch Angles by Vertical — Streaming Media Sales (Worked Example)

A concrete instance of the [pitch-angle framework](../../docs/pitch-angles.md), applied to four real verticals.

## Live events / sports coverage organizations

- **Primary hook:** the gap between an existing web-streaming presence and the absence of a connected-TV presence (Roku, Fire TV, Apple TV)
- **Framing:** additive, not a replacement — position alongside their existing setup

## Content-first organizations (YouTube-first creators, media companies)

- **Primary hook:** audience/data ownership — free platforms sit between the creator and their own viewer relationship
- **Competitive positioning vs. free platforms:** lead with revenue share and audience ownership

## Organizations already running their own VOD platform

- **Primary hook:** reliability, support quality, and centralized management
- **Competitive positioning vs. self-serve/DIY platforms:** operational differentiators — uptime, support responsiveness, tool consolidation

## Public-sector / government access organizations

- **Primary hook:** same connected-TV distribution gap as the live-events vertical, but budget cycles are appropriated and recurring rather than discretionary — this changes the urgency framing, not the core hook
- **Sourcing signal:** organizations already recognized for strong local programming (e.g., industry award winners) tend to have both the content volume and budget tier to justify the conversation
- **Note:** community media organizations are a different buying process than city departments and are pitched separately

## Cross-vertical rule in practice

The vertical template sets the angle; prospect-specific research (recent news, current distribution, business model) is folded in before any draft is generated — the vertical alone is never sufficient.
